B1.6 Claims made in the status report

  1. Brooks' Law is "inviolable" under flat structures: hiring hurt in 100% of 100k runs.
  2. No crossover in 120 ticks: 20 vets lose 95% of their time to meetings forever.
  3. High productivity breaks the law; zero training gives parity.
  4. Mentor pods reach parity; ambassadors restore the J-curve crossover at t=11 (+3,550 points at t=60); synergy is "super-additive".
  5. Next steps: generalise the harness to other models; calibrate with kaiaulu.

Claims 1, 2 and 4 are re-examined in B2. Claim 5 is what Part C does.
