MYTHS34 models

A test-bench for SE theories cast as compartmental system-dynamics models. Each model is a falsifiable conjecture; each is hit by a 10-test V&V bank under N=100 stress-matrix perturbation + a non-parametric two-sample verdict (Cliff's δ + KS + median-ε). The whole battery runs in ~1.3 seconds.

Headline findings

  • F0 — Most published SE theses do not survive proper stats. Of 34 modelled theses, only 13 produce a CONFIRM verdict under N=100 perturbation + stats.same (Cliff's δ 0.195 + KS conf 1.36 + median-ε 0.35σ). 19 dissolve to neutral; 1 flips to REFUTE. The single-shot threshold heuristic was hiding noise as signal.
  • F1 — Sampler choice changes 3 cell classifications. Switching the stress sampler from uniform (adversarial) to triangular (author's-prior-weighted) reclassifies ownership, ossfail, ctxswitch across the 2×2 grid. Robustness is sampler-conditional — another paper-worthy gap in the SE literature, which never declares the sampler.
  • F2 — Boundary-adequacy fails on 5 lifted parameters. Mature OSS projects routinely breach the [lo, hi] ranges declared in sd.py: brooksq.leak_rate exceeds hi=0.5 on 7 of 8 projects (monotone 0.42 → 0.93 across 5 languages); learn.train_rate hits the upper bound on 3 projects. Diagnostic for: bound-tuning is needed.
  • F3 — A few SE theses are project-invariant. debt.pay_rate converges across 5 Java projects (0.36–0.59, 11% spread). Lehman's entropy, Boehm's costchange, Decan's deprot, and the rework cycle all survive both N=100 stats AND the 200-point input/parameter perturbation sweep. These are the few candidates for a universal-law claim in SE.
  • F4 — Brooks's effect is not universal in magnitude. brooks_tax_median varies 11× across 8 projects (Ambari 0.029 → airflow 0.311); on 3 small-sample projects the sign even flips. Net: Brooks's claim holds in direction but not in magnitude. brooksq's quality side shows a 3-project split (Ambari supports, Helix neutral, junit5 refutes).
  • F5 — One thesis flips under perturbation. Merton's successful (Matthew effect) flips from CONFIRM (single-shot) to REFUTE (N=100 + same). The default-run score was on the wrong side of the noise floor — sampling uncovered a sign error in the published thesis encoding.
