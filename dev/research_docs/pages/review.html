Issue 1 · The wrong baseline for the architectures

The modified runs compare architecture X with 10 hires against flat team with 0 hires. But squads and async-docs are already much better than a flat clique with zero hires (1455 and 1334 vs 1050), so the comparison mixes two questions: "is X a better org?" and "does hiring help inside X?".

Issue 2 · Phantom work after completion

In monte_carlo_brooks.py, todo goes negative once the backlog is done while done keeps growing. The baseline's 1050 is 775 done − (−275) todo: 275 items of work that never existed. sd.py clips Done and Todo, so its numbers differ (gap −147.5 vs −697.5). Fix: measure what Brooks claims, which is completion time. That is what brooks_late in Part C did, and what the standard brooks model now does.

Issue 3 · A clamp, not a law, makes "100%"

The formula charges every veteran 20% of their time per newcomer, so each newcomer consumes 2 full-time veterans. Helix data puts train_coef at 0.008 [0, 0.037], about 1/24 of the default (C3).

Issue 4 · Precision is not robustness

Your noise is uniform(0.85, 1.15) (the report says triangular) around the defaults. 100,000 draws from a ±15% box measure the default verdict very precisely; they don't test whether it holds elsewhere. Sampled over the model's declared ranges, Brooks CONFIRMs in about 10% of worlds. The repo's own stress matrix already labelled Brooks fragile (12/200).

Issue 5 · Tests that cannot fail

  • test_boundary_adequacy and test_tipping_points return PASS unconditionally, including "No crossover found" and "none found".
  • forrester_modified.py prints "All physical invariants held" for every architecture whatever the status.

Rule adopted in Part C: every check states its expected result before it runs.

Issue 6 · Hard-coded prose

write_markdown_report always writes that early_hire, break_training and fast_onboarding "break the law". In the table beside it they are CONFIRMED, AMBIGUOUS and CONFIRMED.

Issue 7 · The default project isn't late

Ten veterans at 38.75 items/tick finish 500 items at t≈13, before the t=20 deadline. "Adding manpower to a late project" is tested on a project that was never late. Many "neutral" verdicts just mean both arms finished. brooks_late fixes this with backlog 1000 (about 775 doable by t=20) and outcome = completion tick.

Issue 8 · The comm wall

What survives

  • Inside a genuinely late project, with timing fixed, adding people does lengthen completion in the flat model and in all 7 rival structures (C2). Structure shrinks the harm 8–40×; it doesn't reverse it.
  • Your scenario catalogue and the five architectures were ported into Part C as scenarios (same mechanism, different world) and variants (same world, different mechanism), now compared fairly.
